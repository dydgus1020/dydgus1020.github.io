My research is grounded in **numerical analysis**, with a particular emphasis on **fractional differential equations (FDEs)**. I am interested in developing stable, intuitive, and efficient numerical methods, especially for problems where initial singularities and limited regularity make accurate computation and theoretical analysis difficult.

A recent direction of my work is **AI4Math**—using AI to advance mathematical and scientific computation. My interest in neural networks is driven by numerical challenges rather than by AI itself. In particular, I am interested in combining ideas from numerical analysis with neural-network methods when learning-based approaches can help overcome limitations in classical numerical implementation. My recent interests include physics-informed neural networks, DeepONet, and related operator-learning approaches.

As a longer-term direction, I am also interested in **Math4AI**—using mathematics to advance AI. This includes enriching differential-equation models arising in AI, including fractional formulations, and improving the numerical methods used to solve them.

More broadly, I hope to contribute to **scientific computing** through methods that connect classical numerical analysis with modern neural-network approaches while remaining practically usable.
