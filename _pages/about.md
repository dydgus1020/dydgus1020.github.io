I am a numerical analyst and a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at UNIST. My research began with numerical methods for ordinary and partial differential equations and has gradually expanded toward fractional differential equations, which are now a central part of my work.

More recently, I have been exploring the interaction between numerical analysis and neural networks, including AI4Math approaches such as physics-informed neural networks and DeepONet. In the longer term, I am also interested in Math4AI—bringing mathematical and numerical ideas into the development of AI methodologies.

I value computational methods that are stable, intuitive, efficient, and practically useful.
